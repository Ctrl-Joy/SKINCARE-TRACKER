# 🧴 SkinTracker – Never Let Your Skincare Expire Again

> A skincare expiry tracking web app built with love, care, and a little bit of overthinking. ❤️

## 🌸 The Story Behind This Project

This project started because my girlfriend had a growing collection of skincare products and often found it difficult to keep track of their expiry dates.

Like many skincare enthusiasts, she had products across different categories—serums, moisturizers, sunscreens, cleansers, body care, hair care, and makeup. With dozens of products in rotation, remembering which product expires when became increasingly difficult.

Instead of maintaining spreadsheets or checking labels repeatedly, I decided to build **SkinTracker**—a simple, visual, and user-friendly solution that makes skincare management effortless.

This project is not just a product inventory system.

It's a small gesture of care turned into a practical web application.

---

## ✨ What SkinTracker Does

### 📸 Visual Product Tracking

Store products with:

* Product Image
* Product Name
* Category
* Expiry Date

Because skincare products are easier to recognize visually than by reading long product names.

---

### ⚠️ Smart Expiry Alerts

Products are automatically categorized based on their expiry timeline.

#### 🟢 Safe

More than 5 months remaining

#### 🟡 Expiring Soon

5–3 months remaining

#### 🟠 Critical

2–1 months remaining

#### 🔴 Expired

Past expiry date

---

### 🧴 "Use First" Section

One of the core features of the app.

Instead of making users search through hundreds of products, SkinTracker highlights products that should be prioritized before they expire.

---

## 🎯 Design Philosophy

The goal was not to create a complicated inventory dashboard.

The goal was to answer one simple question:

> "Which products should I use before they expire?"

Everything in the interface is built around simplicity, clarity, and usability.

No clutter.

No unnecessary charts.

No overwhelming analytics.

Just the information that matters.
